# Mallowstead 0.1.4e-candidate1 — Epistemic, Dialogue, Daytime and Agent-Packaging Hardening

## Status

Implementation specification.

Base: `0.1.4d-cognitive` plus the authored-world hotfix already applied on that line.

Target version: `0.1.4e-candidate1`.

This repository copy records the requirements of the supplied candidate specification. World authoring for farm jobs, rewards, and the farmhouse bench is specified separately in `docs/world/0.1.4e-candidate1-farm-work-food-bench.md`.

## 1. Goals

1. Make epistemic source explicit to Character and Mind v3 so testimony is not silently rewritten as firsthand memory or established fact.
2. Reduce conversational echo in multi-character scenes by preferring a distinct contribution or silence over paraphrasing the immediately preceding speaker.
3. Give daytime timelapse enough authored location grounding and tighten the boundary between narration and formal tracked mechanics/movement.
4. Allow one physical sublocation to seat more occupants than may sleep there.
5. Add a developer PowerShell helper that packages a compact working codebase, including the private authored world when present, for coding-agent work.

The patch must preserve the hard rule that free-form narration is never deterministically parsed to infer tracked state or protocol compliance.

## 2. Epistemic model

Mechanical authority and epistemic source are orthogonal.

`worldStateAuthority` continues to answer whether a record may establish tracked canonical state:

- `grounded_event`
- `grounded_result`
- `narrative_only`

`narrative_only` means no authority to create or override tracked mechanical state. It does not mean that everything in the narration is unreal at story level.

Model-facing epistemic source classes are:

- `formal_fact`
- `direct_observation`
- `heard_speech`
- `own_speech`

They are assigned only from already-structured engine channels, never by regex, keyword matching, semantic similarity, prose style, or natural-language fact extraction.

### 2.1 Formal facts

Canonical state and validated formal events/results are mechanically authoritative according to existing state/result rules. When a tracked mechanic exists, its formal result is authoritative; free-form narration cannot substitute for or override it.

### 2.2 Direct observation

Delivered visible/public narrative is story-level witnessed experience even when no formal mechanic exists for the detail. An observer may remember an untracked narrative event as witnessed history, but the engine does not synthesize tracked flags, transfers, locks, placement, money, equipment, movement, or other mechanical state from that prose.

### 2.3 Heard speech

`heard_speech` grounds that the listed speaker uttered the supplied words and the character heard them. Propositions inside the utterance remain testimony/claims. The listener may believe, doubt, repeat, misunderstand, or deliberately lie about them, but the engine does not promote them to canonical truth.

Source attribution must not drift. A character may remember “Zlata mentioned a silvery fish” when that is the supplied source; it may not rewrite this into “Dimka told me,” “I saw it,” “Dimka caught it,” or invented episodic circumstances without independent grounding.

### 2.4 Own speech

`own_speech` grounds that the character itself said the words. It establishes “I said X,” not “X is objectively true.” Boasts, lies, guesses, jokes, promises, and mistakes must not become objective autobiography merely because they are present in recent dialogue.

## 3. Preserve structured narrative and speech channels

AI output already separates `publicNarrative` and `spokenText`; Human free-form RP already passes through the existing `parseStructuredNarrative()` fallback before event creation. Preserve and project those structured fields rather than re-parsing rendered `event.text`.

AI contract:

- `publicNarrative` contains visible narration/behavior only;
- `spokenText` contains words actually spoken only;
- AI must not place `*inline narration*` inside `spokenText`;
- visible gestures/actions accompanying speech belong in `publicNarrative`.

New `narrative_input` recipient/model projections preserve the two channels and expose explicit epistemic parts. For the speaker's own projection the speech part is `own_speech`; for other recipients it is `heard_speech`. Grounded formal events/results expose `formal_fact` while retaining structured event/result data and `worldStateAuthority`.

`combineInteractionObservations()` may still group one interaction for conversational convenience, but must preserve all individual epistemic parts rather than collapsing direct narrative, testimony, and formal result into one semantically ambiguous blob.

## 4. Recent dialogue

Model-facing `context.recentDialogue` adds:

```json
{
  "epistemicStatus": "heard_speech",
  "speakerId": "zlata",
  "speakerName": "Zlata",
  "text": "The silvery one as long as his arm?"
}
```

When `speakerId` is the current actor, status is `own_speech`. Persisted recent-dialogue records need no new field because the status is deterministic at projection time.

Prompt guidance must state that the utterance is certain but its content is not automatically an occurred fact; source/time/place may not be invented; testimony attribution must be preserved; claims of firsthand seeing/hearing/being-told require supplied evidence for that specific experience/source.

## 5. Character prompt contracts

All Character stages that can produce public narration/dialogue, including ordinary decisions and grounded-result reactions, must receive high-salience separation equivalent to:

```text
=== CANONICAL / FORMAL FACTS ===
Canonical state and validated formal engine results are mechanically authoritative.

=== EVENTS YOU DIRECTLY OBSERVED ===
Delivered narrative was actually visible/experienced and may be remembered as witnessed story history in untracked domains, but narration cannot create or override tracked mechanical state.

=== SPEECH / TESTIMONY ===
You definitely heard the listed speaker say these words (or said them yourself when marked own_speech). Propositions inside speech are claims, not automatically objective facts. Preserve the actual source.
```

Existing wording must not imply that `narrative_only` is categorically non-factual. Its limitation is tracked world-state authority.

### 5.1 Anti-confabulation

Character guidance must forbid silently converting testimony into firsthand memory, swapping the actual source, inventing when/where a prior conversation occurred, or adding absent episodic detail merely for smooth prose. Plausible reconstruction must be framed as inference/uncertainty or omitted. Deliberate deception remains allowed only as motivated in-character deception under the existing grounding contract.

### 5.2 Anti-echo

A character should not merely restate, paraphrase, endorse, or revoice the immediately preceding speaker. A reaction should add a distinct thought, question, disagreement, consequence, action, joke, concern, memory, or character-specific perspective. When there is nothing meaningful to add, prefer a genuine no-op (`action:null`, `spokenText:null`, no unnecessary public narration). Functional repetition remains allowed for surprise, clarification, exact quotation, emphasis, orders, misunderstanding, etc. Do not implement similarity scoring or prose deduplication.

## 6. Verbatim provenance

New verbatim observations may persist optional structured `epistemicParts` with the same four allowed types. Mixed-channel records may contain multiple parts. Parts are produced only from known structured engine channels; legacy prose is never parsed to synthesize missing provenance. Old verbatim records without the field remain valid. `worldStateAuthority` remains separate and keeps its existing mechanical meaning. Legacy `text` remains for compatibility/debug.

## 7. STM provenance

STM may persist optional compact `epistemicSources`, an array of descriptors such as:

```json
[
  { "type": "direct_observation" },
  { "type": "heard_speech", "sourceCharacterId": "zlata" }
]
```

Requirements:

- types match verbatim epistemic types;
- speech descriptors may/should carry a valid existing `sourceCharacterId`;
- duplicate identical descriptors normalize away;
- do not persist every source event ID;
- summaries still preserve source semantics for individual claims;
- a coarse source array never authorizes blurring which claim came from whom;
- legacy STM without the field remains valid and is not rewritten merely to add provenance.

STM Utility guidance must preserve firsthand vs testimony stance, keep heard testimony attributed, treat own speech as an utterance rather than proof, avoid invented circumstances, preserve strongest supplied grounding, and keep `narrative_only` non-authoritative mechanically without declaring direct narrative unreal. Proposal schemas, validators, normalization, repartition, commit, persistence, migration, save/load, and portable mind transfer must tolerate/preserve the optional field.

## 8. LTM source preservation is semantic only

Do not add persistent `epistemicSources` to LTM. LTM consolidation receives STM source metadata as context and must preserve epistemic stance in durable summary text without increasing certainty merely because material is being consolidated. No LTM schema migration is introduced.

## 9. Save compatibility

- old verbatim may lack `epistemicParts`;
- old STM may lack `epistemicSources`;
- old LTM is unchanged;
- migration must never parse old prose to infer missing provenance;
- new observations/consolidations use the new contracts;
- normal fresh-authored-world plus runtime-overlay migration remains authoritative;
- absence of an optional provenance field is valid state, not a repair condition.

## 10. Daytime timelapse authored grounding

Reachable/planner location context must include authored grounding, at minimum location `description` and relevant visible sublocation `name`, `publicText`, `selfText`, capabilities, capacity/affordance fields, while retaining existing timelapse actions/study/beds. Prefer existing projection helpers where practical. Do not reveal hidden/undiscovered locations or inaccessible secret data. No farmhouse-specific branch.

## 11. Daytime narration boundary

Sponsored jobs and relevant free-NPC daytime narration must clearly state:

- canonical movement between named locations belongs to the engine/formal timelapse layer and occurs outside narration;
- a round narrates activity only at its selected `locationId`;
- do not narrate departing for, walking toward, entering, arriving at, or already being inside another canonical location;
- leave travel to the engine;
- local texture/untracked actions within the current worksite are fine;
- if an intended activity requires a tracked mechanic and no supplied formal timelapse/study/action contract can perform it, choose another narratable activity instead of simulating the missing tracked action in prose.

Tracked domains include item/container placement or transfer, locks/passage state, writing/writable content, filling/transforming/consuming tracked items, money, equipment, canonical location/sublocation movement, sleeping, and other supplied formal abilities/actions. This is prompt hardening only; narration is not post-scanned for forbidden claims.

## 12. Generic `sleepCapacity`

Authored sublocations may optionally declare:

```json
"sleepCapacity": 1
```

Rules:

- meaningful only when `capabilities` includes `sleep`;
- positive integer;
- `<= capacity`;
- omitted value defaults to ordinary `capacity`, preserving old worlds/saves.

Normal occupancy still uses `capacity`. Starting sleep additionally requires that the number of other currently sleeping occupants is below effective sleep capacity. Apply this consistently to ordinary formal sleep, daytime/night timelapse sleep placement, and bed exposure/selection helpers. Awake occupants consume ordinary capacity but not sleep capacity; sleepers consume both. Do not add a posture state machine; existing `sleeping` remains authoritative.

## 13. Developer agent-package helper

Add `tools/package-for-agent.ps1` for a compact development snapshot. It is not a public release artifact and must not derive its source list from Git state because ignored `data/world.private.json` should be included when present.

Use `.agent-packages/` as a local output directory and add it to `.gitignore`. Never recursively package that directory.

Use an explicit whitelist including development-relevant `data/`, `docs/`, `editor/`, `src/`, `tests/`, `tools/`, root `AGENTS.md`, README/player README, `LICENSE`, build/test shell/batch entry points, and other small root source/config files actually needed to understand/build/test the project.

Exclude `.git/`, `.build/`, `dist/`, `model-contract-bench/`, `.agent-packages/`, archives, logs, backups, IDE metadata such as `.vscode/` unless genuinely required, and generated/transient junk outside the whitelist.

The helper creates the output directory, fails readably, avoids overwrite collisions, prints resulting archive path/basic summary, and never modifies source files.

## 14. Version/build metadata

Product version is `0.1.4e-candidate1`. Generated build info must be regenerated through the existing tooling rather than hand-edited when owned by the build pipeline.

## 15. Tests

Deterministic coverage must include:

- structured narrative/speech projection into separate epistemic parts;
- recipient `heard_speech`, actor `own_speech`, narrative `direct_observation`, formal `formal_fact`, and preserved authority;
- combined interaction preserving all parts;
- recent-dialogue heard/own status;
- legacy observations/STM without new fields;
- STM source normalization, invalid types/source IDs, add/upsert/repartition/save/load/portable preservation, and LTM no-schema-expansion;
- Character/STM/LTM prompt contract presence for epistemic separation, anti-confabulation, anti-echo, speech-only `spokenText`, and hearsay-to-firsthand prohibition;
- timelapse authored grounding and discovery boundaries;
- narration boundary and unavailable-tracked-mechanic instructions;
- `sleepCapacity` default/validation/awake occupancy/one sleeper plus awake/rejected second sleeper/ordinary and timelapse consistency;
- package helper whitelist and `.gitignore` protection.

## 16. Explicit non-goals

This candidate does not parse narration into canonical facts, add a semantic fact checker, reject prose via regex/similarity, add a proposition truth database, make testimony objectively true/false in engine state, add multi-location farm work zones or a farmer scheduler, add a generic posture state machine, reconstruct legacy provenance, persist structured provenance on LTM, or alter/include model-contract-bench packaging.

## 17. Acceptance summary

The candidate is acceptable when:

1. Character context unmistakably distinguishes canonical/formal facts, direct observed narration, and speech/testimony from structured channels rather than prose parsing.
2. Direct narrative can remain witnessed story history in untracked domains without acquiring mechanical authority.
3. Heard testimony remains attributed through immediate context and STM/LTM consolidation rather than becoming firsthand autobiography.
4. Old saves/memory records remain valid without synthesized provenance.
5. Crowded dialogue prefers distinct contribution or silence instead of echo.
6. Daytime models receive authored grounding and cannot pre-narrate canonical travel or unavailable tracked actions.
7. Mixed-use sleep positions separate ordinary capacity from sleeping capacity.
8. The PowerShell helper produces a compact agent ZIP including private authored world data while excluding Git/build/benchmark junk.
9. The normal test suite passes and the version reports `0.1.4e-candidate1`.
