# Mara's Arcane Knowledge Slab — Indexed Lore Articles

**Status:** Implemented world authoring
**Engine:** `docs/engine/abstract-study-authored-knowledge-entries.md`

## Purpose

Mara's **Slab of Full Arcane Knowledge** keeps its existing open-ended abstract-study behavior, but selected setting subjects now have deterministic canonical reference articles embedded in the slab's `abstract_study` action authoring.

These entries are intended to grow into a lore encyclopedia that NPCs can actually consult and later reason from.

## Chugaister

Entry ID:

```text
chugaister
```

Current aliases include English/transliteration variants and Cyrillic forms, including:

```text

чугайст*
```

The article describes the Chugaister as a rare shaggy forest being generally friendly or indifferent to people, hostile to predatory forest wraiths, associated in some traditions with challenging travelers to dance, and repeatedly associated with broad trampled clearings. The article deliberately does **not** reveal the location of Mallowstead's secret glade or assert that a Chugaister is currently there.

## Outer-World Construct Hypothesis

Entry ID:

```text
outer_world_construct_hypothesis
```

Current aliases include:

```text
otherworld*
other world
another world
foreign world
outside reality
world outside
constructed world
construct hypothesis
simulation*
```

The canonical article attributes an unfashionable hypothesis to a small school among the **archmages of Veyra**: experienced reality might be a self-sustaining magical construct, or in a stranger formulation the dream of such a construct. On that hypothesis, entities originating outside it may sometimes enter while carrying properties native magic can observe but not fully explain.

No known divination, planar experiment, soul-reading, or causal test can currently prove or disprove the hypothesis, because a sufficiently complete construct cannot be distinguished from an underlying reality by available magic. It therefore remains philosophical speculation rather than accepted cosmology.

The article intentionally uses fantasy-world framing rather than treating modern "simulation theory" as established vocabulary inside the setting.

## Fallback behavior

Questions that do not match an indexed article continue through the slab's existing deterministic abstract-study progression. Indexed lore therefore supplements the slab rather than converting all of its open-ended study behavior into a finite encyclopedia.
