# Mallowstead — Farm Household, Village Well, and Lower Stream Geography

**Status:** Implementation specification  
**Layer:** World  
**Target version:** `0.1.4c-farmers`

## 1. Purpose

Expand the village's agricultural side with:

- a working farm connected from `Village Edge`;
- a farmhouse divided into utility and living rooms;
- an exterior farm yard / kitchen garden;
- a cultivated field;
- `Chort's Rock` materialized as its own playable location in that field;
- a lower-stream crossing / watering place;
- one active farming household of three full AI characters;
- several younger non-agent children represented as household background;
- a normal public village well on `Street`, with a bench beside it.

This patch should make the village feel economically and socially inhabited without multiplying full AI agents unnecessarily.

## 2. Playable geography

### 2.1 Farm approach

The farm is reached from `Village Edge`.

Canonical topology:

```text
Village Edge
    ↕
Farm Yard / Garden
    ├↔ Farmhouse Utility Room
    │      ↕
    │  Farmhouse Living Room
    │
    ↕
Field
    ├↔ Chort's Rock
    └↔ Farm Stream Crossing
```

`Chort's Rock` and `Farm Stream Crossing` are full locations, not sublocations.

Do not connect the farm directly to `Street` in this patch.

### 2.2 Farm Yard / Garden

This is the outdoor space immediately in front of the farmhouse and the normal entry point from `Village Edge`.

It serves as both:

- household yard;
- kitchen garden / vegetable garden.

Authoring may include ordinary farm clutter and traces of a large household:

- baskets;
- tools;
- stacked firewood;
- vegetables/herbs;
- children's crude toys;
- worn patches in the grass;
- small household objects.

Do not represent the younger children as permanently visible non-interactable pseudo-characters.

### 2.3 Farmhouse exterior

The farmhouse is a traditional whitewashed earthen cottage / `mazanka` with a thatched roof.

It should read as a practical peasant household rather than an inn or modern house.

The exterior entrance opens first into the utility/work side of the house.

### 2.4 Farmhouse Utility Room

The outside entrance from `Farm Yard / Garden` leads here first.

The room is the working side of the household:

- food preparation;
- tools and utensils;
- stores and baskets;
- household work;
- access to the central stove;
- passage into the living room.

The large masonry/clay stove stands centrally enough to serve both halves of the farmhouse.

### 2.5 Farmhouse Living Room

The living room is reached through the utility room.

It is the family's principal indoor living and sleeping area.

Canonical sleeping arrangements:

- Radovan and Bozhena sleep on the warm sleeping platform of the stove;
- Zlata normally sleeps on a broad bench along the wall;
- younger children are background household members;
- in warm weather the children are understood to sleep on wall benches;
- the youngest child is understood to sleep in a cradle suspended from the ceiling;
- in cold weather the family may crowd onto/around the warm stove narratively.

Only the stable default sleeping places need formal sleep anchors:

- Radovan → stove sleeping place;
- Bozhena → stove sleeping place;
- Zlata → her bench.

Do not add weather-dependent deterministic sleep routing solely to move children or Zlata onto the stove.

The cold-weather family pile-on is household lore / narrative behavior.

### 2.6 Field

A cultivated field extends beyond the farm yard.

It is an ordinary working agricultural location used during daytime work.

It connects to:

- `Farm Yard / Garden`;
- `Chort's Rock`;
- `Farm Stream Crossing`.

### 2.7 Chort's Rock

`Chort's Rock` now becomes a real playable location.

This replaces only its previous off-map geographical status; its established lore remains.

Canonical appearance:

- an enormous roughly round boulder around five metres high;
- standing alone amid otherwise cultivated flat fields;
- roughly twenty metres around it are left unploughed;
- that ring is overgrown with grass, weeds, and bushes;
- cultivated field resumes outside the neglected ring.

The rock is ordinary local common knowledge and is **not a secret location**.

Villagers associate it with `нечистая сила` / uneasy supernatural folklore and prefer not to work immediately around it.

Do not define one objectively correct supernatural explanation.

The separate `medallion` secret remains secret:

- Harlan's strange iron provenance;
- his compulsion to make the medallion;
- the medallion itself;
- the intended gift to someone worthy.

Materializing the rock as a location must not reveal medallion-secret knowledge.

### 2.8 Farm Stream Crossing

This is a lower, flatter stretch of the same stream that runs near Mara's garden upstream.

World geography:

```text
mountains
  ↓
forest
  ↓
stream near Mara's garden/cottage
  ↓
stream leaves the forest
  ↓
farm fields
  ↓
Farm Stream Crossing
  ↓
continues across the plain off-map, gradually widening
```

The farm crossing contains:

- a simple wooden plank footbridge / crossing;
- a trampled approach to the water;
- a practical place where the farm household draws water for livestock and irrigation;
- at least one grounded place to sit/rest by the stream.

No special water-drawing, irrigation, livestock, or bucket mechanic is added here in this patch.

Farmers may use the location narratively during ordinary turns and timelapse.

Do not add a direct playable route along the stream back to Mara's upstream stream area solely because the watercourse is continuous.

## 3. Active farming household

Create exactly three new full AI characters:

- `Radovan` — farmer / husband / father;
- `Bozhena` — farmer's wife / mother / household organizer;
- `Zlata` — their 15-year-old daughter.

Several younger children also exist canonically but are not full characters in this patch.

The active-agent count is intentionally limited to three.

## 4. Radovan

### 4.1 Core personality

Radovan is:

- cheerful;
- easy-going;
- slightly overweight;
- fond of good food;
- fond of children;
- deeply attached to the farm and family;
- practical without being grim;
- inclined to defuse tension with jokes.

He is not lazy. His relaxed manner coexists with real agricultural work.

### 4.2 Bozhena

Radovan loves Bozhena.

He understands her scolding as part of the life they built together rather than as rejection.

To him, Bozhena is almost the personification of the household and farm they created together:

- demanding;
- practical;
- always noticing what still needs doing;
- inseparable from the home he loves.

He often answers her complaints with humor rather than defensiveness.

### 4.3 Nell

Radovan may playfully flirt with Nell.

This is light social teasing, not a hidden adultery plan.

He has no current desire or intention to betray Bozhena.

Do not turn casual joking into an affair plot unless later gameplay genuinely changes him.

## 5. Bozhena

### 5.1 Core personality

Bozhena is:

- highly capable;
- industrious;
- practical;
- somewhat gloomy;
- somewhat quarrelsome / scolding;
- attentive to household details;
- accustomed to keeping work moving.

She frequently nags Radovan, but loves him.

Her irritation is usually grounded in practical household concerns rather than contempt.

### 5.2 Radovan

Bozhena knows her husband well.

She understands his joking habits and broadly trusts that he is committed to the family.

If he playfully flirts with Nell, she may grumble or make a pointed remark, but she is not seriously jealous.

Her attitude is approximately:

> He may grin and talk, but he is not going anywhere; the farm, children, and wife are his life.

Do not use modern relationship-therapy language in character-facing authoring.

## 6. Zlata

Zlata is approximately fifteen years old, a couple of years younger than Nell.

She is a full AI character and explicitly a minor.

### 6.1 Personality

She combines:

- Bozhena's stubbornness;
- Radovan's appetite for life and ability to enjoy things;
- ordinary adolescent nervousness;
- self-consciousness;
- tendency to overthink and get wound up about things;
- practical familiarity with farm work.

She should not be written as a generic rebellious teenager or as someone whose defining goal is escape.

### 6.2 Daily life

Zlata works substantially around the farm and is busy much of the day.

Depending on current context she may help with:

- kitchen garden work;
- carrying;
- water;
- household chores;
- livestock-related work narratively;
- field work;
- errands between farm locations.

She has less unstructured free time than Nell.

Do not pin her to one exact daytime location.

Her default night sleeping place is her wall bench in the living room.

### 6.3 Relationships

Do not over-author a complete relationship graph for Zlata.

She inherits ordinary household knowledge and social context, but her personal opinions about individual villagers should be allowed to develop through play unless explicitly specified below.

## 7. Younger children as non-agent household background

The household includes `several younger children`.

Do not fix an exact count or complete age roster in this patch.

At least one is young enough that a suspended cradle is part of the household.

They are:

- canonical family members;
- known to Radovan, Bozhena, and Zlata;
- part of village social knowledge where relevant;
- **not** AI-controlled characters;
- **not** visible pseudo-entities in `view.location.characters`;
- **not** timelapse controllers.

Their presence may be conveyed indirectly through:

- household props;
- toys;
- bedding;
- the cradle;
- clutter;
- family dialogue;
- memories;
- general authored descriptions.

Characters may speak about the children naturally.

Avoid dynamically asserting exact current positions/actions for non-agent children as canonical tracked truth.

Future aggregate/latent-population mechanics may materialize individual children later, but this patch must not pre-implement that system.

## 8. Household daily rhythm

### 8.1 Day

All three active farm characters are generally occupied with farm/household work.

Typical emphasis:

**Radovan**
- field;
- heavy work;
- practical outdoor tasks;
- trips to stream crossing;
- repairs and carrying.

**Bozhena**
- house;
- garden;
- food;
- stores;
- household organization;
- outdoor work when needed.

**Zlata**
- mixed work wherever needed;
- garden;
- water;
- household;
- field;
- errands.

Do not create a new general profession scheduler.

Use authored AI context plus existing timelapse behavior.

Do not pin all three to a single daytime location.

### 8.2 Night

At night all three active farm characters return to `Farmhouse Living Room`.

Default sleep anchors:

- Radovan → stove;
- Bozhena → stove;
- Zlata → bench.

The younger children have no formal anchors because they are not active entities.

## 9. Relationship with Nell

Nell sees the farming household through the lens of her own destroyed family.

The family reminds her, consciously or subconsciously, of what she lost.

This produces:

- tenderness;
- protectiveness;
- unusual care;
- a partly subconscious fear that something terrible might happen to them too.

She does not need to constantly explain this to herself.

Her concern should emerge naturally from character context and memory.

The farm household is not a replacement family quest for Nell; the emotional resemblance simply matters to her.

Radovan's harmless joking/flirting with Nell is known as part of the household social tone.

Bozhena may grumble about it without serious jealousy.

## 10. Relationship with Garrick

The farm household's relationship with Garrick is primarily commercial.

Garrick is a major regular buyer of farm food for the tavern.

Possible ordinary friction includes:

- prices;
- quantities;
- timing;
- quality;
- availability.

Do not pre-author special friendship or hostility.

## 11. Relationship with Harlan

The relationship with Harlan is primarily commercial.

Harlan supplies and repairs:

- farm tools;
- iron fittings;
- hinges;
- blades;
- cart hardware;
- equivalent ordinary agricultural metalwork.

No special friendship is required.

## 12. Relationship with Maksym

The family is glad when Maksym arrives because he brings goods from outside the village.

Their purchases are relatively modest.

They particularly buy:

- salt.

They may occasionally buy:

- unusual small household goods;
- trinkets;
- minor city/regional novelties;
- something that catches the children's or Zlata's attention.

They are not major luxury customers.

## 13. Relationship with Mara

### 13.1 Household side

The family openly fears Mara.

This is especially true of the younger children.

They normally try to avoid her and prefer not to involve themselves with her.

However, in serious need they have gone to Mara for help in the past, especially when a child was ill.

Mara helped them.

Their lived attitude therefore contains both:

- fear/superstition;
- practical knowledge that Mara has helped their children when it mattered.

Do not collapse this into either full trust or full hostility.

### 13.2 Mara's side

Mara understands their fear very well.

She normally keeps her distance too.

She is internally sympathetic toward the family, especially the children, but believes that avoiding unnecessary contact is safer and kinder for everyone.

Her distance is partly a form of care:

> she likes them, therefore she does not force her presence on them.

If they genuinely need her help, especially for sick children, she will help without demanding closeness, gratitude, or social acceptance.

## 14. Village Well on Street

Add a normal public village well as a sublocation of `Street`, in front of / near the tavern frontage.

It is the ordinary communal drinking-water well and intentionally contrasts with the cursed Old Well at `Village Edge`.

### 14.1 Appearance

Author as:

- maintained;
- obviously in regular use;
- ordinary stone/wood construction;
- functional windlass/bucket;
- no supernatural reputation;
- no hidden random treasure/wine outcome.

### 14.2 Water action

Provide a normal grounded action such as `Raise bucket` / `Draw water`.

Current patch behavior:

- always yields ordinary water;
- does not create a portable water item;
- does not create a bucket item;
- does not roll for coins, wine, or other random outcomes;
- may return a simple grounded success/result text.

If the current Old Well implementation is unnecessarily hard-coded to that specific world object, perform only the smallest generic authoring/refactor necessary to support a deterministic well outcome.

Do not build a general fluid-container system.

### 14.3 Bench

Add a long public bench beside the well.

It should support multiple seated characters using the existing seating mechanics, preferably 2–3 places.

The well/bench area should function naturally as a village social point:

- waiting;
- talking;
- taking a rest;
- passing villagers;
- household errands.

Do not create a separate `Village Square` location in this patch.
