# Mallowstead 0.1.4 — Medallion Secret

**Status:** World-authoring implementation specification  
**Layer:** World  
**Target version:** `0.1.4`  
**Dependency:** generic transparent keyed-container capability from the companion engine spec

## 1. Purpose

Add the authored `medallion` secret centered on Harlan and a strange iron medallion.

This is world content using existing secret authoring plus the new generic transparent-container capability.

Do not add a soul mechanic, medallion powers, special voices, possession, supernatural reactions, or any other mechanical effect in this patch.

## 2. Secret boundary

Add authored secret:

```text
medallion
```

Everything specific to Harlan's medallion mystery belongs to this secret.

This includes:

- the medallion item;
- its display frame;
- its key;
- Harlan's medallion-specific authored knowledge/character context;
- the strange iron provenance;
- Harlan's unexplained compulsion to make it;
- his conviction that he must eventually give it to someone worthy.

`Chort's Rock` itself does **not** belong to the secret.

## 3. Chort's Rock common knowledge

Chort's Rock is ordinary local common knowledge among village residents.

Current lore:

- a huge round boulder roughly five metres high;
- standing alone in otherwise flat cultivated fields;
- roughly twenty metres around it is left unploughed;
- grass, weeds, and bushes grow around the stone;
- fields resume beyond that neglected ring;
- villagers avoid working the immediate ground around it because of superstition;
- local stories associate it with `нечистая сила` / unclean supernatural forces;
- folklore may be contradictory and need not define one objective supernatural explanation.

The rock is **not a canonical location yet**.

Do not add an exit, location record, pathfinding target, discoverable-location state, or map topology for it in 0.1.4.

A real location may be authored later when farm/field geography is added.

## 4. Public-vs-secret knowledge boundary

Ordinary villagers may know Chort's Rock and its reputation.

They must not automatically know:

- Harlan found unusual iron there;
- the medallion was made from that iron;
- Harlan felt compelled to make the design;
- Harlan intends to give it to a worthy person.

Those are `medallion`-secret facts.

## 5. The medallion

Add a small iron medallion.

Physical/artistic description:

- iron rather than precious metal;
- excessively neat and finely worked for an ordinary village-smith product;
- the whole surface carries a dense complex engraving;
- the design is interlaced animal-geometric ornament;
- it evokes old northern knotwork and branching/trident-like motifs without copying any known real/local sign;
- the pattern may feel old or strangely familiar, but no villager can identify it as a known symbol;
- the craftsmanship visibly required a disproportionate amount of time and concentration.

It should look like something Harlan exhausted himself making, not rough folk craft.

## 6. Harlan's experience

Secret-authored Harlan context should establish:

- he found a strange piece/ingot of iron near Chort's Rock;
- after finding it he somehow knew he had to make this particular medallion;
- he cannot explain why;
- he may remember the process as though he knew each next line without needing a proper sketch;
- he is certain the finished object must eventually be given to someone he personally considers worthy;
- he does not know the reason for that conviction;
- he is evasive when directly questioned.

Do not reveal an objective supernatural cause.

Do not state that a soul, spirit, demon, spell, curse, or artifact intelligence is definitely responsible.

## 7. `Worthy` is model-owned judgment

There is no deterministic worthiness mechanic.

Do not add:

- relationship threshold;
- quest flag;
- reputation score;
- morality stat;
- authored whitelist;
- deterministic trigger.

Harlan's Character model decides organically whether someone is worthy based on his personality, memories, beliefs, relationships, observations, and current context.

Until he decides so, the medallion remains in its display.

If he chooses to give it, he uses ordinary grounded item transfer after obtaining physical access to it.

## 8. Display frame

Place the medallion in a small glazed, lockable display frame mounted on the wall of the smithy's front forge room.

Fiction:

- wood-and-metal frame;
- glass front;
- unusually elaborate for a village where good glass is scarce/expensive;
- made or acquired because Harlan treats this object with unexplained seriousness.

The display is not hidden in the living room or a chest.

It must be positioned/described so the medallion is **visible from the entrance / ordinary forge-floor position**.

A visitor entering the smithy should not need to search the building to learn that the conspicuous medallion exists.

## 9. Mechanical authoring of the frame

Use an inventory-bearing sublocation/container associated with the forge-floor visual context.

It must:

- contain the medallion;
- require a specific ordinary key item instance through existing `requiredKeyItemId`;
- set the companion engine capability `transparent: true`;
- remain reachable/visible from the ordinary forge-floor context as authored.

Without the key:

- the medallion is canonically visible;
- it is not physically actionable.

With the key:

- normal item access applies.

No medallion-specific engine branch is allowed.

## 10. Key

Add a dedicated key for the glazed medallion frame.

The key begins in **Harlan's direct inventory**.

It is not Garrick's key.

The key is an ordinary transferable key item under existing mechanics.

If Harlan loses or transfers it, physical access changes naturally through the generic keyed-container rule.

## 11. Secret filtering

The medallion item, frame, frame key, and medallion-specific Harlan authored knowledge/context should be owned by `secretId: "medallion"` wherever supported by the authored secret system.

Disabling the secret should remove this content cleanly without removing:

- Harlan himself;
- the smithy;
- ordinary Harlan characterization;
- common village knowledge of Chort's Rock.

## 12. Existing Harlan characterization

Preserve Harlan's established ordinary character:

- practical village blacksmith;
- dry humor;
- slight arrogance;
- ordinary mundane forge work;
- Garrick mutual dislike/respect;
- friendly non-romantic familiarity with Nell;
- wary but practically respectful history with Mara.

The medallion secret augments him; it does not replace his ordinary personality with an artifact-obsessed mystic.

## 13. World continuity notes

Update developer/world continuity documentation so it clearly separates:

### Common lore
Chort's Rock exists in the surrounding cultivated fields and is known to villagers.

### Secret lore
Harlan's iron provenance, compulsion, medallion, display frame, and intended future gift.

The continuity note must also explicitly state that Chort's Rock is not yet a simulated location.

## 14. Soul mechanic deferred

Explicit non-goal for 0.1.4:

Do not implement any actual soul-related mechanic yet.

In particular do not add:

- soul storage;
- soul transfer;
- artifact consciousness;
- stat effects;
- medallion speech;
- magical observation effects;
- possession;
- bonding;
- automatic reaction hooks.

These may be designed later without rewriting the basic medallion provenance.

## 15. Regression coverage

Add authored-world/release tests covering at least:

1. `medallion` exists as an authored secret;
2. Chort's Rock is not owned by that secret;
3. no Chort's Rock location is created;
4. local common-knowledge authoring mentions the rock appropriately;
5. the medallion exists only when its secret is enabled;
6. the frame exists only when its secret is enabled;
7. the dedicated frame key begins in Harlan's direct inventory;
8. the frame is keyed and transparent;
9. medallion visibility is available from the ordinary smithy entrance/forge-floor context;
10. disabling `medallion` leaves Harlan and the smithy intact;
11. no soul/artifact-power mechanic is added.

## 16. Non-goals

This world patch does not:

- create Chort's Rock as a location;
- create farms/fields;
- define the true supernatural origin of the iron;
- define an objective meaning for the engraving;
- add deterministic worthiness;
- add a soul mechanic;
- introduce a special medallion action.
