# Mallowstead 0.1.3 Playtest — Public World Finalization

**Status:** Implementation specification  
**Scope:** public authored world polish, Maksym age portrayal, fresh-world starting-scene regression contract, world continuity documentation, tests  
**Baseline:** `0.1.2e-candidate3`  
**Target version:** `0.1.3-playtest`  
**Acceptance profile:** `public`

---

## 1. Purpose

Make the final authored-world adjustments before the public Mallowstead playtest.

This is deliberately a small world patch. The current world, schedules, secrets, merchant lifecycle, jobs, and starting-evening composition are already broadly correct. The goal is to remove one misleading age cue from Maksym and lock the intended fresh-world opening scene against regression.

---

## 2. Public-world scope

The acceptance target is the canonical public authored world:

```text
data/world.json
```

Captain Price and Price-specific private-world content are not part of `0.1.3-playtest` acceptance.

Do not spend this patch extending, balancing, or repairing Price-specific authoring. The private build may be tested after the public playtest version is produced.

Shared world prose may be kept consistent in a locally available private source when convenient, but no private-only change is required for this patch to be accepted.

## 3. Maksym: remove the numerical adult-age cue

Remove the explicit **`early thirties`** age wording from Maksym's authored description/context.

The problem is not that the old number is internally impossible. The problem is that a numerical adult age is unnecessary for play and can strongly bias younger players' interpretation of the character's apparent age.

Maksym should read as:

- **young**;
- already experienced and road-tested;
- somewhat weathered by travel and danger;
- someone who has seen serious trouble and ugly things on the road;
- still energetic, curious, and drawn toward travel and life;
- clearly not framed as an older man.

The intended contrast is:

```text
young + experienced + somewhat weathered
```

not:

```text
older because experienced
```

### 3.1 `playerDescription`

Keep the visible description compact and immediately legible as a young person who has already lived through difficult roads.

A suitable semantic shape is:

> A young road merchant, moustached and weathered by travel, but still clearly young. He carries himself with easy confidence and the measured caution of a man accustomed to weapons and dangerous roads.

Exact final prose may be polished during implementation, but it must not restore a numeric age range.

### 3.2 `aiDescription`

Remove the explicit age number while preserving the important character contrast already present in the current authoring:

- younger than Garrick and Harlan;
- visibly driven by youth, curiosity, appetite for life, and the next road;
- nevertheless has already seen ambushes, ugly deaths, desperate people, and bad country;
- treats danger seriously without becoming a swaggering veteran caricature.

A suitable opening semantic shape is:

> You are Maksym the Wagoner, a young traveling merchant and road adventurer. You are younger than Garrick and Harlan, still visibly driven by youth, curiosity, appetite for life, and the pull of the next road, but you have already seen ambushes, ugly deaths, desperate people, and enough bad country to take danger seriously.

Keep the rest of his established personality, merchant behavior, weapon caution, wagon knowledge, social stance, and road motivation unchanged unless a tiny grammatical adjustment is required by the age edit.

### 3.3 World continuity notes

Update the Road Merchant entry in `data/world-lore.md` so it no longer says `in his early thirties` or otherwise reintroduces a concrete adult age range.

The continuity note should preserve the same intended image: young, energetic, already experienced with violence/hardship, cautious because of experience rather than because of age.

### 3.4 Do not replace one unnecessary number with another

Do not change `early thirties` into another numeric age, decade, or narrow range.

The authored fact needed by the game is qualitative youth plus experience, not a birth-year estimate.

## 4. Fresh-world opening scene is Monday Evening

Preserve and explicitly test the current intended start:

```text
Monday · Evening
```

Do **not** revert fresh worlds to Morning.

Ordinary time progression remains unchanged: the player begins in Evening, can play that evening, and the existing overnight/daytime systems advance coarse time through their normal entry points.

## 5. Fresh-world tavern composition

A newly created public world starts with the following canonical composition.

### 5.1 Traveler

The Human-controlled Traveler begins at the tavern entrance:

```text
location: tavernEntrance
sublocation: tavernEntranceFloor
```

The Traveler is approaching/entering an evening scene that is already in progress rather than spawning seated among the established villagers.

### 5.2 Mara

Mara the Hedge Witch is already in the Common Room at the first table:

```text
location: commonRoom
sublocation: commonRoomTableOne
```

She is part of the lived-in opening tavern scene.

### 5.3 Maksym

Maksym the Wagoner is already in the Common Room at the second table:

```text
location: commonRoom
sublocation: commonRoomTableTwo
```

He starts with one ordinary tracked mug already filled with ale, as established by the existing fresh-world evening placement spec.

This is authored initial state, not a scripted first-turn purchase/pour/movement sequence.

### 5.4 Nell

Nell is present in the Common Room in her ordinary waitress/barmaid working position:

```text
location: commonRoom
sublocation: commonRoomFloor
```

Do not move her to a chair merely to make every named character literally seated. Her role in the opening scene remains active tavern staff.

### 5.5 Garrick

Garrick the Innkeeper is behind the bar:

```text
location: bar
sublocation: barBehindCounter
```

He begins in his normal working position.

## 6. Maksym's existing lifecycle remains unchanged

The opening tavern placement is a **fresh-world bootstrap**, not a rewrite of his awayable schedule.

Preserve the existing authored/runtime behavior:

- the starting weekday is Monday;
- fresh world begins Monday Evening with Maksym already present;
- later arrival opportunities and travel cooldown use the existing awayable lifecycle;
- his ordinary workplace remains Market Square;
- his ordinary sleeping place remains the wagon;
- his current filled mug is a normal tracked item using existing drink mechanics;
- leaving, deferring departure, restocking, and absence behavior remain unchanged.

Do not add a special first-turn routine that moves him from Market Square to the tavern or purchases his ale.

---

## 7. Save / migration behavior

Do not teleport established saves to match the fresh-world opening tableau.

The intended starting positions above are for a newly created current-authored world. Existing compatible saved runtime position/state continues to win under the project's normal migration rules.

In particular, do not move an existing saved Maksym, Mara, Nell, Garrick, or Traveler solely because the authored fresh-world defaults changed or were reasserted in this patch.

The Maksym prose update is authored descriptive data and should follow the project's normal current-authored-description-wins migration behavior.

---

## 8. Economy is not part of this world pass

Do not use this final world patch to stabilize the economy.

Leave current merchant stock/value hooks and current bargaining/transfer behavior intact unless a separate correctness bug is discovered during implementation.

Specifically do not:

- add a universal price list;
- broadly expose exact market prices as common knowledge;
- rebalance Maksym's whole stock;
- introduce a dedicated Trade/shop action;
- expand natural-wage commerce authoring merely because the README wording is being corrected by the engine companion spec.

Economy stabilization is deferred to the next version after playtest feedback.

---

## 9. Character-specific abilities remain unchanged

This world patch does not normalize character capabilities.

Mara's unique authored abilities, Chuhaister's unique authored abilities, and any other existing character-specific ability authoring remain deliberate character distinctions.

The companion engine guideline about Human/AI affordance parity is **per character** and must not be interpreted as a reason to copy these abilities onto the Traveler, Maksym, Nell, Garrick, Harlan, or anyone else.

## 14. Acceptance Criteria

The world side of `0.1.3-playtest` is complete when:

1. The public authored world still begins on **Monday · Evening**.
2. The fresh-world opening positions are Traveler at the tavern entrance, Mara at Common Room table one, Maksym at Common Room table two with a filled ale mug, Nell working on the Common Room floor, and Garrick behind the bar.
3. Maksym is described qualitatively as young but experienced/weathered, with no `early thirties` or replacement narrow numeric age range in public authoring/continuity notes.
4. Maksym's established merchant, road-adventurer, weapon-caution, social, schedule, workplace, sleep, restock, and awayable-lifecycle characterization/behavior remain unchanged.
5. Existing saves are not teleported to the fresh-world opening positions.
6. No economy expansion or rebalance is introduced.
7. No Captain Price content is reintroduced into the public world, and private/Price authoring is not a playtest blocker.
8. Relevant public-world regression tests pass.
