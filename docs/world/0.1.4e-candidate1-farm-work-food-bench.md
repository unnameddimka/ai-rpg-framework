# Mallowstead 0.1.4e-candidate1 — Farm Work, Food Rewards and Farmhouse Bench

## Status

Implementation specification for authored world content.

Engine/protocol/tooling changes are specified in `docs/engine/0.1.4e-candidate1-epistemic-daytime-hardening.md`.

Base: `0.1.4d-cognitive` plus the authored-world hotfix already applied on that line.

Target version: `0.1.4e-candidate1`.

Apply equivalent shared authored-world changes to public and private Mallowstead sources. Generated world artifacts are regenerated through the existing pipeline rather than hand-edited.

## 1. Goals

Add two distinct full-day sponsored farm jobs using existing generic `world.dayActivities` machinery:

- outdoor/general farm work with Radovan;
- household/farmstead work with Bozhena.

Both use `farmYard` as their single canonical worksite in this candidate. Also add ordinary edible farm/household reward definitions and make Zlata's wall bench usable as ordinary seating while retaining one-person sleeping capacity.

Do not add multi-location work zones, pasture, barns, new farm locations, or farmer profession scheduling.

## 2. Generic daytime-job assumptions

Reuse the existing sponsored-job mechanisms for Morning offers, Accept/Decline, five-round daytime timelapse, sponsor + Traveler worksite binding, sponsor settlement, `sponsor_items`, and formal reward delivery after successful completion. No Radovan-, Bozhena-, or farm-specific engine settlement branches.

Repeated quantities use one reward row with `count > 1`; do not duplicate the same definition ID merely to represent repeats.

## 3. Canonical worksite

Both new jobs use:

```json
"workLocationId": "farmYard"
```

Do not add `workLocationIds`, work areas, automatic canonical movement among farm locations, or narrated travel among `farmYard`, `farmField`, `farmhouseUtility`, `farmStreamCrossing`, etc. The yard is the canonical anchor for a compressed day of work around the immediate farmstead. Narration may cover plausible local labor around the house/yard/garden/animals/cart/fence/woodpile/roof exterior without claiming formal entry into another named canonical location. No pasture exists in this candidate.

## 4. Radovan job

Suggested ID: `radovanFarmAssistance`.

Required generic identity:

```json
{
  "id": "radovanFarmAssistance",
  "kind": "sponsored_job",
  "sponsorCharacterId": "radovan",
  "workLocationId": "farmYard"
}
```

Player-facing name may be `Help Radovan on the farm`.

Radovan may offer ordinary paid farm assistance in Morning through the existing personality-sensitive offer rules. Payment is produce, not gold. Neutral Travelers who reasonably ask for work are normally acceptable, while memories/relationships/conflict/context may justify refusal. Do not spam the offer every morning without conversational reason.

Narration may draw from fence repair, cart loading/unloading, carrying feed, yard livestock care/handling, garden/vegetable labor, carrying tools/baskets/stakes/fodder/produce, and similar outdoor farm assistance. Radovan directs the work and the Traveler is auxiliary labor. Do not claim canonical movement into another named farm location, invent pasture travel, or create/hand over payment during work narration.

### 4.1 Radovan reward

Use generic `sponsor_items`, `minTotal: 2`, `maxTotal: 3`, exact allowed pool:

- `turnip`
- `onion`
- `buckwheatGroats`
- `apple`

Repeated quantities are allowed through `count`. No gold salary.

## 5. Bozhena job

Suggested ID: `bozhenaFarmsteadAssistance`.

Required generic identity:

```json
{
  "id": "bozhenaFarmsteadAssistance",
  "kind": "sponsored_job",
  "sponsorCharacterId": "bozhena",
  "workLocationId": "farmYard"
}
```

Player-facing name may be `Help Bozhena around the farmstead`.

Bozhena may offer full-day practical household/farmstead assistance in Morning. Payment is household food rather than coin. Neutral Travelers who ask reasonably can normally be accepted; memories, relationships, suspicion, conflict, and scene context may justify refusal. Keep the tone practical rather than forcing cheerfulness/softness.

Narration may draw from chopping/splitting/stacking/carrying firewood, minor exterior repairs, farmstead-side thatch/roof work, feeding/milking livestock where plausible around the immediate yard, carrying water/feed/buckets/supplies, garden/vegetable labor, sorting ordinary farm products, and similar near-house chores. Garden work may overlap Radovan's domain; Bozhena's emphasis remains household organization, maintenance, food/animal chores, and domestic farm labor. Do not narrate canonical entry into farmhouse rooms or another named location and do not create/hand over payment during work narration.

### 5.1 Bozhena reward

Use generic `sponsor_items`, `minTotal: 2`, `maxTotal: 3`, exact allowed pool:

- `eggs`
- `farmCheese`
- `breadLoaf`

Repeated quantities are allowed through `count`. No gold salary.

## 6. New food item definitions

Add seven ordinary definitions if equivalent canonical definitions do not already exist:

1. Turnip
2. Onion
3. Buckwheat groats / groats
4. Apple
5. Eggs
6. Farm cheese
7. Bread loaf

They are real inventory items, not abstract reward tokens. Each has a stable suitable `familyId`, grounded food/produce tags, is non-equippable and non-fillable unless an existing pattern truly requires otherwise, and is edible through the existing formal `consumeAction` mechanic. Standalone foods whose whole instance is consumed use `resultType: "remove"` and appropriate action label, AI prerequisites/description, feedback text, and public text.

Do not invent reusable bowls/plates for standalone produce. Groats/eggs should use a natural transferable farm-payment unit. Do not add merchant pricing, wage-good sale rules, recipes, cooking transformations, nutrition, spoilage, production simulation, or other economy systems unless an existing validator requires a harmless default.

## 7. Zlata's wall bench: sit + sleep

Modify the existing physical `farmhouseLiving.sublocations.zlataBench`; do not create overlapping seat/bed sublocations.

Required semantics:

```json
{
  "capacity": 2,
  "sleepCapacity": 1,
  "capabilities": ["sleep"]
}
```

Thus two awake characters may occupy/sit on it; one may sleep while at most one other occupant sits; two may not sleep there simultaneously.

Revise wording so awake occupancy is not automatically described as lying down. Keep the name `Zlata's bench`; public text should retain that it is Zlata's usual sleeping place with blanket/personal things while also being a broad wall bench used for sitting. Use a natural seating `enterLabel`, neutral/seated `selfText`, and neutral occupant wording compatible with the separate authoritative `sleeping` flag. Do not add a posture state machine.

## 8. Public/private parity

Apply equivalent shared changes to:

## 9. Migration

Use existing save migration: fresh target authored world plus compatible saved runtime overlay. Older compatible saves receive the new day-activity definitions, item definitions, and target-authored mixed-use bench fields while preserving compatible runtime character/mind/inventory state. No one-off migration is required merely to add these authored definitions, and no reward instances are manufactured into old inventories.

## 10. Tests

### 10.1 Activity definitions

Verify both activities exist, are `sponsored_job`, use the correct sponsor IDs, both use `farmYard`, both use `sponsor_items` totals 2–3, exact reward pools match authoring, and generic day-activity validation accepts them with no sponsor-specific branch.

### 10.2 Reward settlement

With deterministic/model-stub sponsor output verify:

- valid total 2 succeeds;
- valid total 3 succeeds;
- repeated quantity through one definition/count succeeds;
- disallowed definition fails settlement validation;
- below-2 and above-3 totals fail;
- success creates real item instances in Traveler inventory;
- no reward item exists during the five work narration rounds.

### 10.3 Food definitions

Verify all seven definitions validate, formal consume is offered while accessible in actor inventory, standalone consumption removes the item through existing formal behavior, and no reusable dish is created.

### 10.4 Bench

Verify `capacity === 2`, `sleepCapacity === 1`, sleep capability remains, seating wording no longer forces lying down, and generic engine tests enforce one sleeper maximum.

### 10.5 Public/private generation

Verify public world generation contains all shared changes and private world generation contains those same shared changes while preserving private-only content; generated artifacts use normal tooling and existing release-profile checks pass.

## 11. Explicit non-goals

No pasture, barn/stable locations, livestock simulation, multi-location worksite sets, per-round farm movement, crop growth/harvest simulation, cooking recipes, infinite farm inventories, merchant pricing, profession schedules, jobs for Zlata/younger children, or general posture system.

## 12. Acceptance summary

World content is complete when:

1. Radovan can generically offer a Morning full-day farm job anchored at `farmYard`, paying 2–3 produce items from turnip/onion/groats/apple.
2. Bozhena can generically offer a distinct Morning farmstead/household job anchored at `farmYard`, paying 2–3 items from eggs/cheese/bread.
3. Rewards use generic sponsored-item settlement and may repeat a definition via `count`.
4. All seven reward types are tangible edible definitions using existing formal consume mechanics.
5. Job narration stays plausible at the farmstead without asserting canonical travel into other named locations.
6. Zlata's physical bench seats two and supports formal sleep, but allows only one sleeper.
7. Public/private authored worlds remain in parity for shared content and generation/tests pass.
