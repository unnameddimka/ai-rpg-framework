# Mallowstead 0.1.4 — Nell Morning Freedom and Tavern Relationship Context

**Status:** World-authoring implementation specification  
**Layer:** World  
**Target version:** `0.1.4`

## 1. Purpose

Clarify Nell's daily social/work rhythm and the material/social relationship between Nell and Garrick.

This is primarily authored character/lore behavior.

Do not create a new professional scheduler for Nell.

The existing deterministic evening routine anchor remains the boundary guarantee.

## 2. Morning: Nell is generally free

During Morning, Nell is not required to remain inside the tavern merely because she works there in the evening.

She may:

- walk around;
- visit people;
- pursue ordinary personal interests;
- remain at the tavern if she feels like it or has a reason.

Morning is her relatively free period.

Do not add a deterministic Morning anchor that pins her to the tavern.

## 3. Breakfast

The tavern already supports Morning food service.

If Nell happens to be present at the tavern, she may use the ordinary serving mechanics to help with breakfast.

She is not required to be present solely because breakfast exists.

Garrick remains capable of handling ordinary tavern cooking/service himself.

## 4. Garrick may ask when genuinely needed

Garrick may socially ask Nell to help during Morning when there is real work or an unusual need.

He should not invent chores simply to keep her under control or prevent her from leaving.

No new formal coercion/action mechanic is required.

This is Character-model behavior grounded in their established arrangement.

## 5. Daytime

During the daytime span, Nell should understand that she needs to return to the tavern in time to prepare for the evening opening/work period.

Her coarse-time behavior may include:

- returning toward the tavern;
- tidying/preparing;
- collecting/organizing mugs or dishes;
- making the room ready;
- ordinary pre-opening work.

Do not require a new daytime profession scheduler solely for this.

The normal timelapse Character behavior can express the activity.

## 6. Evening boundary guarantee

Preserve Nell's existing evening routine anchor:

```text
commonRoom / commonRoomFloor
```

At the Day -> Evening boundary, the existing deterministic routine-anchor mechanism ensures she is back in her ordinary working position.

This is the canonical guarantee.

The model may prepare/return naturally during daytime, but the engine does not depend on narration for the final placement.

## 7. Evening

During Evening, Nell resumes her established waitress/barmaid role:

- moving around the tavern;
- watching the room;
- collecting mugs;
- carrying items;
- helping serve food/drink;
- responding to patrons.

No change to her established ordinary evening role is intended.

## 8. Material arrangement with Garrick

The relationship must not be framed as a modern equal-wage employment contract.

Nell effectively works in exchange for:

- food;
- shelter;
- safety;
- a stable place in the tavern household.

By modern standards this is a materially unequal, semi-servile dependency.

The authoring should acknowledge that inequality rather than romanticizing it into a 21st-century employer/employee arrangement.

At the same time, it is not authored as constant hostility, imprisonment, or daily coercion.

## 9. Stable situational trust

Their arrangement has developed real stability.

Garrick:

- does not expect Nell to suddenly disappear;
- trusts that she will return and do the evening work;
- does not need to keep her physically supervised every morning.

Nell:

- does not currently plan to run away without warning;
- has no obvious safer or better destination;
- broadly accepts the tavern as her present home;
- may have vague hopes for a different future without treating escape as an active quest.

This trust is situational and exists within an unequal material relationship.

## 10. Garrick's paternal feeling

Garrick has genuine paternal/guardian-like affection for Nell.

He likes having those feelings even if he rarely articulates them sentimentally.

His internal reasoning should remain period/world appropriate rather than modern therapeutic language.

Suitable semantic framing:

> She works hard in the evening. If there is no useful work, let her go about her business. A cheerful Nell is good for the room.

He may rationalize giving her freedom in practical business terms:

- customers respond well to her good mood;
- her smiles and liveliness improve the tavern atmosphere;
- pointless control would make her resentful or dull;
- there is no profit in inventing work that does not need doing.

The practical explanation can coexist with genuine affection.

## 11. Garrick authoring updates

Extend Garrick's AI description / initial known facts / relationship summary as appropriate so he knows:

- Nell's compensation is food, roof, safety, and belonging rather than ordinary wages;
- he trusts her to return for evening work;
- he normally allows her free Morning time when there is no real task;
- he may ask for help when the tavern genuinely needs it;
- he does not manufacture chores merely to assert control;
- he has paternal feelings toward her;
- he can justify the arrangement to himself in practical tavern-business terms.

Avoid making him sound like a modern HR manager.

## 12. Nell authoring updates

Extend Nell's AI description / initial known facts / relationship summary as appropriate so she knows:

- she works for food, shelter, and safety rather than a normal wage;
- the arrangement is materially unequal;
- Morning is generally hers unless real help is needed;
- she is expected to be dependable about returning/preparing for Evening;
- she regards the tavern as home for now;
- she trusts Garrick not to invent arbitrary work merely to keep her close;
- she does not currently plan to vanish without warning.

Her future remains open; do not author a deterministic life plan.

## 13. World continuity notes

Record the social arrangement in world continuity documentation:

- materially unequal food/shelter-for-work dependency;
- genuine mutual situational trust;
- Garrick's paternal feeling;
- Nell's current acceptance of the tavern as home;
- Morning freedom;
- daytime return/preparation;
- Evening work.

The documentation should explicitly avoid both misleading extremes:

```text
not modern egalitarian employment
not constant coercive captivity
```

## 14. No new scheduler

Do not add:

- exact work hours;
- a Morning location anchor;
- generic shifts;
- a profession scheduler;
- payroll;
- wage mechanics;
- mandatory breakfast presence;
- a deterministic “return at round N” rule.

Use authored Character context plus the existing evening routine anchor.

## 15. Save/migration

These are current authored personality/relationship/routine clarifications.

Existing saves should preserve developed runtime mind/relationship state according to existing migration precedence.

Do not overwrite developed saved relationship evolution merely to restate the fresh-world baseline.

The existing routine anchor remains authored current-state data.

## 16. Regression coverage

Add tests covering at least:

1. Nell keeps the existing Evening anchor at `commonRoom/commonRoomFloor`;
2. no Morning anchor pins Nell to the tavern;
3. daytime completion still returns her to the Evening working position through existing routine-anchor handling;
4. Morning serving remains mechanically possible when she is present;
5. no new professional scheduler is introduced;
6. current authored Garrick/Nell descriptions and initial mind establish food/shelter-for-work rather than modern wage employment;
7. authored context establishes Morning freedom and reliable Evening return;
8. authored context establishes stable trust and Garrick's paternal/practical motivation.

## 17. Non-goals

This patch does not add:

- a generic employment system;
- wages for Nell;
- a coercion mechanic;
- a romance arc;
- a fixed future for Nell;
- a deterministic morning itinerary;
- a broad tavern-management simulation.
